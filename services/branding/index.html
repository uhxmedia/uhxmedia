04 / FAQ

Questions before we start?

A few common questions about UHX MEDIA branding projects.

What does a branding project include?

It can include brand strategy, positioning, visual identity, logo direction, messaging and brand guidelines depending on your requirements.

Do you work with new businesses?

Yes. UHX MEDIA can help new businesses establish a clear identity as well as help existing businesses improve or refresh their brand.

Can branding and web design be combined?

Yes. Branding, web design, UX and digital marketing can be combined into one connected project when the business requires it.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. UHX MEDIA is based in India and works remotely with businesses in India and international markets.
