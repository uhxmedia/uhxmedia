05 / FAQ

Questions before we start?

A few quick answers about UHX MEDIA UX research services.

What is UX research?

UX research is the process of studying users, behaviours, needs and experiences to inform better product and digital decisions.

Why is UX research important?

Research helps teams understand users and identify experience problems before investing heavily in design or development.

Do you conduct user interviews?

Yes. User interviews can be included when they are appropriate for the research objectives and available audience.

Do you offer usability testing?

Yes. Usability testing can be used to evaluate websites, interfaces, prototypes and digital experiences.

Can UX research be combined with web design?

Yes. Research can directly inform information architecture, UX decisions, interface design and website strategy.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. UHX MEDIA is based in India and works remotely with clients in India and worldwide.
